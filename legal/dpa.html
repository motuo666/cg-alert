Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

This DPA forms part of the agreement between CG Alert (“Processor”) and the customer (“Controller” / “Customer”). It governs how Processor handles personal data on behalf of Customer. This document is provided as a baseline template; final execution may include negotiated terms, schedules and subprocessors.

1. Scope

CG Alert may process limited personal data to deliver the Service: contact details for vendor/commercial stakeholders, escalation context you submit, and suppression/bounce data to respect unsubscribe and complaint requests. We do not require end-user/customer PII, and you should not upload regulated data unless explicitly agreed in writing.

2. Roles

  • Customer is the data controller and determines the lawful basis for processing.
  • CG Alert is the data processor and will process personal data only on Customer’s documented instructions.

3. Security Measures

We maintain reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure or access. We limit access to personnel with a need to know.

4. Subprocessors

We may use vetted subprocessors (e.g. hosting, email delivery, storage). A current list of subprocessors is available on request. We will notify Customer before materially changing subprocessors where required by law or contract.

5. Data Subject Rights

Where legally required, we will assist Customer in responding to requests to access, correct, delete or restrict personal data, to the extent we are technically able to and such assistance is commercially reasonable.

6. International Transfers

If we transfer personal data across borders, we will use appropriate safeguards (e.g. SCCs or other mechanisms allowed by applicable law) as agreed in the fully executed Order Form.

7. Audit / Compliance

Upon reasonable written request and subject to confidentiality, we will provide information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and applicable data protection laws, and will allow for audits as mutually agreed in the Order Form.

8. Termination / Deletion

At termination or upon written request, we will delete or return personal data we processed for you, unless retention is required by law, audit defense, or dispute resolution. We may keep suppression logs (who unsubscribed / complained) as required to ensure we do not email them again.
