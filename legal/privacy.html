Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CG Alert (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects information in connection with the CG Alert service (“Service”).

1. What We Collect

(i) Account / Billing Data you provide: contact name, work email, company name, billing info.
(ii) Operational Data: which vendors you ask us to monitor; escalation notes you submit via intake.
(iii) Email Routing Data: bounce/complaint/unsubscribe signals so we can honor suppression and avoid re-contacting people who opted out.
(iv) Public Vendor Evidence: captures of public-facing pricing pages, SLA pages, DPAs, subprocessor lists, terms and similar materials from vendors.

2. How We Use It

We use your data to:

  • Provide and improve the Service (monitor vendors, generate evidence packets, alert you of changes).
  • Route alerts and escalation material to the correct contact at your company.
  • Honor unsubscribe / suppression. If someone asks us to stop, we stop and we permanently mark them suppressed.
  • Process billing / invoicing / account support.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share information with subprocessors strictly to run the Service (e.g. email delivery, storage, secure hosting). Upon request, we will provide an up-to-date subprocessor list under NDA or via the DPA.

4. Data Retention

We keep vendor change evidence as part of the product archive. Your internal escalation notes and intake submissions are retained while you remain a customer and for a reasonable period after termination for audit and compliance. We retain suppression/bounce logs as long as needed to ensure we do not email suppressed contacts again.

5. Your Rights

You can request deletion of internal notes you submitted via intake, correction of billing contact info, or suppression of specific contacts. Email ops@cg-alert.com to open that request. If laws in your jurisdiction require additional rights (access/erasure/etc.), we will comply.

6. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. No system is 100% secure. We recommend you do not send us secrets or regulated personal data in intake notes unless contractually agreed.

CG Alert

Contact: ops@cg-alert.com
