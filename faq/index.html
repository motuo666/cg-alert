FAQ

What sources do you monitor?

Public vendor pages: Pricing, Terms/SLA, DPA, Subprocessors, Status/Security. We respect robots and sitemaps.

How noisy are alerts?

Two-stage de‑noise. Target <10% false positives. We prioritize material changes.

What does “evidence-backed” mean?

Each alert includes URL, timestamp, and SHA256 hash. You can cite these in procurement/legal threads.

How do I receive alerts?

Email only. No calls.
