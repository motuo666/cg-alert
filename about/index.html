About

CG Alert monitors public vendor pages (Pricing, ToS, DPA, Sub‑processors, Status) and delivers high‑signal alerts with verifiable evidence cards.

We serve procurement, legal, and RevOps teams who need timely, defensible change evidence without meetings or manual checks.

  • Evidence includes URL, excerpt, timestamp, and hash.
  • Noise reduced via two‑stage confirmation.
  • Email & Slack delivery; Enterprise via intake form.
