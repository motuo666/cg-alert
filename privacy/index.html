Privacy

We store only what is needed to deliver alerts (contact email and delivery preferences). We do not sell or share your data. Unsubscribe tokens are HMAC‑signed.

Data we process

  • A) Public web change fragments used to build evidence cards
  • B) Business email addresses for notifications, subscriptions, and billing

Principles

  • No sale / no sharing of personal data
  • Data minimization: we retain only evidence fragments and canonical URLs
  • Unsubscribe / deletion: every email includes an unsubscribe link; or email us at ops@cg-alert.com
  • Security: each evidence card includes a timestamp and SHA256 hash for auditability
