We received your payment and the details you entered during Stripe checkout (company name, billing email, and top vendors). No tickets, no kickoff calls.

What happens next

  • Monitoring starts for the vendors you listed. We prioritize your stack over public sample vendors.
  • Evidence is collected whenever we detect material changes in Pricing, Terms/DPA, SLA, Subprocessors, or Security pages.
  • Digests are generated on the schedule attached to your plan (weekly by default). They ship to the email you used at checkout.

You can update your vendor list or add extra recipients at any time using the optional update form. Changes will apply to upcoming digests; there is no manual approval step.
