How CG Alert builds these reports

  1. We continuously capture key vendor pages (pricing, terms, DPA, security, status, etc.).
  2. Each capture is normalized, fingerprinted (SHA256) and stored with full diff history.
  3. We detect meaningful changes, score their impact and generate human-readable summaries.
  4. For paying customers, we attach ready-to-send escalation language for renewals and legal reviews.
