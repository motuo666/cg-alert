Privacy

Plain-language summary of what we collect, why, and how to make us delete it.

Data sources

  • We monitor only public-facing vendor pages (Pricing, Terms/ToS/MSA, DPA, Subprocessors, Status). We never monitor behind login or paywall.
  • For each observed change we store: URL, timestamp (UTC), short text excerpt (≤300 chars), and a cryptographic hash of the diff for audit.
  • We do not ingest customer data, credentials, or internal system traffic. Monitoring is passive, public-source only.

Outbound email / alerts

  • We may email Procurement, Legal Ops, Finance, RevOps, Security, etc., if we believe you handle vendor risk or renewal negotiations.
  • Every outreach comes from a monitored domain with SPF/DKIM/DMARC, with a working reply-to address.
  • Every alert links to an evidence card (timestamp + hash + URL) so you can document internally.

Retention & deletion

  • Evidence cards (URL, timestamp, hash, short excerpt) are snapshotted and backed up daily for integrity / audit.
  • We will remove specific evidence excerpts that include personal data or confidential info that should not be public.
  • Opt-out / suppression: email [email protected] with your domain / role and “OPT-OUT” in the subject. We target 72h removal from future outreach.

Security contact

To report abuse, request deletion, or raise a security concern: email [email protected]. You can also use /.well-known/security.txt.
