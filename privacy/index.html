Privacy

We store only what is needed to deliver alerts (contact email and delivery preferences). We do not sell or share your data. Unsubscribe tokens are HMAC‑signed.

What we process

  • Public web content captured as change snippets for evidence.
  • Business email data required for delivery (notifications, subscriptions, billing).

Principles

  • No sale or sharing of personal data.
  • Data minimization: we keep only the snippet required for evidence and its source URL.
  • Unsubscribe/Delete: every email includes an unsubscribe link; you can also write to ops@cg-alert.com.
  • Security: evidence is hashed (SHA256) and timestamped for auditability.
