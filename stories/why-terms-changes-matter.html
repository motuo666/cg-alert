Why vendor terms changes matter more than a logo refresh

Brand changes are loud. Contract changes are quiet — and usually more expensive.

Most teams notice when a vendor changes their logo or pricing page layout. Very few notice when the same vendor quietly updates their indemnity cap, data processing addendum, or right to unilaterally change features.

CG Alert treats each of those changes as evidence: timestamped, vendor‑scoped, and linked back to the original source document.

From marketing copy to risk surface

We track legal sections like Terms, Privacy, Subprocessors, Security, and DPAs as first‑class monitoring targets. When a change is detected, it goes through a two‑stage confirmation process before it ever shows up in your dashboard.
