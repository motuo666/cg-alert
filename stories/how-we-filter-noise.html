How CG Alert filters noise and focuses on material changes

Not every text change is a risk. The hard part is deciding what to ignore.

We combine path‑level targeting, vendor‑specific rules, and a confirmation queue to avoid alert storms from cosmetic edits.

Small layout tweaks or typo fixes are usually filtered out. Pricing moves, new subprocessors, or changes to data usage clauses are promoted as “material changes” and surface on your reports and digests.

This is the foundation for a moat you can explain to customers: we’re not just watching URLs — we care about what changed and why it matters.
